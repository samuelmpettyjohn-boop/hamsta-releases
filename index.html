HamstaTV™

Clip saving and trimming for your games, made to go with Hamsta.

Press your save key and HamstaTV keeps just the moment you want. Trim it, then send it in any chat with the + button, or put it on your profile for friends to watch.

Free clips are up to 2 minutes at 720p 60 fps, with 2 GB of clip storage and 1 clip on your profile. Turn it on in Hamsta under Settings, Hamsta Clipper, and Hamsta installs it and keeps it updated for you.

How to join

  1. Get Hamsta. Download it and open it, or click Use in browser.
  2. Paste your join link. Use the one-time link a friend sent you, and pick a username.
  3. Save your login code. Keep it somewhere safe: it's how you sign in again, on any PC or browser.

For push-to-talk in games and the best screen share, use the desktop app.
