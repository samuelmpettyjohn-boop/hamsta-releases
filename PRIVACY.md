# Hamsta Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Last updated October 12, 2026

This policy explains what the Hamsta™ app and the HamstaTV™ clip app (the "software", made by Samuel M Pettyjohn, "the developer", "we") collect, where it goes and what you can do about it.

## The short version
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we don't sell your data.
- Your account, messages and files live on the Hamsta server you join, which is run by its host. The developer doesn't receive them unless the developer is your server's host.
- Calls, cameras and screen shares are sent live through your server and are not recorded.
- You can download a copy of your data or delete your account yourself, in Settings, My Account.

## Who runs your server
Every Hamsta server is run by its host on their own computer. The host can access everything stored on their server, can see the server's activity log and decides how long things are kept. Only join servers whose host you trust.

## What is stored on your server
- Your account: your username, profile photo, the date you joined, whether you're the host, and a one-way hash of your login code (not the code itself).
- Whether you have Hamsta+, and until when.
- Your profile banner, profile color and "About me", if you've set them.
- When you agreed to the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy: which version and the time.
- Your friends list, friend requests, and the DMs and group chats you're in, including group names and photos.
- Your block list: the people you've blocked.
- The messages you send in channels, DMs and group chats.
- The files you upload, with their names and sizes.
- Reports: when someone reports a user or a message, the report stores who reported, who was reported, the reason given, and a copy of the reported message (its text and the names of its files), so the host can review it even if the message is deleted.
- Your username and the IP address you connect from, in the server's activity log.

These are not end-to-end encrypted, so the host can read them.

## What isn't stored
- Voice, video, cameras and screen shares are sent live through the server to the people in your call. They're encrypted on the way and are never recorded or saved.
- Remote control input (mouse and keyboard) is passed straight to the person who agreed to it and is never saved.

## What stays on your computer
- Your login code, encrypted by Windows for your user account.
- Your settings, such as devices, volumes and layout.
- Files you download, saved where you choose.
- HamstaTV (optional) saves your clips on your computer. They stay there unless you share them.

## Who can see what
- Everyone on your server can see your username, profile photo, whether you're online and which call you're in.
- While you have Hamsta+, everyone on your server can also see your profile banner, profile color, "About me" and H+ badge.
- Messages in a server's channels can be seen by everyone on that server. DMs and group chats can be seen by their members, and by the host, who can access the server's storage.
- People in a call can hear you, and can see your camera and screen share when you turn them on.
- Your block list is only shown to you. The host can also access it through the server's storage.
- Reports are only seen by the host of your server.

## Security
Connections to your server are encrypted (TLS 1.3) and locked to that server's certificate, so nobody else can pretend to be it. Updates are signed by the developer. No system is perfectly secure, so don't share anything that would seriously harm you if it leaked.

## Keeping and deleting data
- Data stays on your server until it's deleted.
- When you delete a message, it's removed from the server along with its files.
- You can delete your account yourself in Settings, My Account, Delete account. It removes your account, every message you sent, the files you uploaded and your DMs from that server, and removes you from your group chats. It can't be undone. The host's own account can't be deleted this way.
- Group chats keep the messages other people sent in them.
- If someone reported you or one of your messages, the copy saved in that report is kept for the host to review, even after the message or your account is deleted.
- Deleting your account doesn't clear the server's activity log, which the host controls.
- You can also ask your server's host to delete your account. Hosts can delete content and accounts at any time.

## Getting a copy of your data
You can download your data from your server in Settings, My Account, Download my data. It gives you a file with your account info, your friends, your chats, the messages you sent and a list of your uploads.

## Children
Hamsta isn't for children under 13, or under the higher minimum age in their country (for example, 16 in some EU countries), and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you're a parent or guardian and think your child is under that age and has an account, ask the server's host to remove it, or contact us below.

## Link previews
When a message has a link, your server visits that page to show a preview (its title, description and picture) and passes the picture on to you. The linked site sees your server's IP address, not yours. Previews are only kept in the server's memory for a few hours and aren't saved. You can turn previews off in Settings, Chat.

## Connections the app makes
Hamsta only connects to the Hamsta server you join. Updates come from that server too. It doesn't send your data anywhere else. The one exception: when you press play on a YouTube video in a chat, the video loads from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com), which then sees your IP address and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

## Changes
We may update this policy. If the changes are important, the app will ask you to agree again before you continue.
