# Hamsta Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

This policy explains what the Hamsta app and the HampterTV clip app (the "software", made by Samuel M Pettyjohn, "the developer", "we") collect, where it goes and what you can do about it.

## The short version
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we don't sell your data.
- Your account, messages and files live on the Hamsta server you join, which is run by its host. The developer doesn't receive them unless the developer is your server's host.
- Calls, cameras and screen shares are sent live through your server and are not recorded.

## Who runs your server
Every Hamsta server is run by its host on their own computer. The host can access everything stored on their server, can see the server's activity log and decides how long things are kept. Only join servers whose host you trust.

## What is stored on your server
- Your account: your username, profile photo, the date you joined, whether you're the host, and a one-way hash of your login code (not the code itself).
- Your friends list, friend requests, and the DMs and group chats you're in, including group names and photos.
- The messages you send in channels, DMs and group chats.
- The files you upload, with their names and sizes.
- Your username and the IP address you connect from, in the server's activity log.

These are not end-to-end encrypted, so the host can read them.

## What isn't stored
- Voice, video, cameras and screen shares are sent live through the server to the people in your call. They're encrypted on the way and are never recorded or saved.
- Remote control input (mouse and keyboard) is passed straight to the person who agreed to it and is never saved.

## What stays on your computer
- Your login code, encrypted by Windows for your user account.
- Your settings, such as devices, volumes and layout.
- Files you download, saved where you choose.
- HampterTV (optional) saves your clips on your computer. They stay there unless you share them.

## Who can see what
- Everyone on your server can see your username, profile photo, whether you're online and which call you're in.
- Messages in a server's channels can be seen by everyone on that server. DMs and group chats can be seen by their members, and by the host, who can access the server's storage.
- People in a call can hear you, and can see your camera and screen share when you turn them on.

## Security
Connections to your server are encrypted (TLS 1.3) and locked to that server's certificate, so nobody else can pretend to be it. Updates are signed by the developer. No system is perfectly secure, so don't share anything that would seriously harm you if it leaked.

## Keeping and deleting data
- Data stays on your server until it's deleted.
- When you delete a message, it's removed from the server along with its files.
- To delete your account, ask your server's host. Messages you posted may stay unless they're deleted too.
- Hosts can delete content and accounts at any time.

## Children
Hamsta isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you're a parent or guardian and think your child under 13 has an account, ask the server's host to remove it, or contact us below.

## Connections the app makes
Hamsta only connects to the Hamsta server you join. It doesn't send your data anywhere else.

## Changes
We may update this policy. If the changes are important, the app will ask you to agree again before you continue.

## Contact
